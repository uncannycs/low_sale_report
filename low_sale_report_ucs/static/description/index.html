See It In Action

1. Configure Defaults in Settings

Under Settings > Sales > Low Sales Report, set the Default Report Type, Default critical level (absolute quantity), and Default analyzed period so the wizard opens pre-filled every time.

2. Available Right in the Sales Menu

"Low Sale Report" sits as its own menu in the Sales app top bar, alongside Orders, To Invoice, Products, Reporting, and Configuration, so your team finds it instantly.

3. Report Filter Wizard

Choose the Report Type (variant/template), Report Period (Week, Month, Year or Custom), set the Critical Level (absolute quantity), and optionally filter by Product Categories or Sales Teams, then click "Show in Odoo" or "Export as xlsx".

4. Pivot View in Odoo

Click "Show in Odoo" to open the underperforming products directly in a native pivot view, complete with Total and Sold Quantity, ready to group, expand, and drill into.

5. Styled Excel (xlsx) Export

Click "Export as xlsx" for a ready-to-share Excel file with a LOW SALES REPORT title block, report period and filter metadata, KPI summary cards (underperforming products, total sold quantity, total revenue), and a colour-coded, banded product table with a grand total row.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Low Sale Report module.

1. Is Low Sale Report compatible with Odoo 17 Community & Enterprise?

Yes, absolutely! The module is 100% compatible with Odoo 17 Community, Enterprise, and Odoo.sh cloud environments.

2. Where can I find the Low Sale Report menu in Odoo?

"Low Sale Report" sits as its own top-level menu in the Sales app main navigation bar alongside Orders, To Invoice, Products, Reporting, and Configuration.

3. How is a product identified as "low performing"?

You define a Critical Level (absolute quantity threshold). Any product or variant whose total sold quantity falls below this threshold in the chosen period will be listed in the report.

4. Can I analyze sales by product variant as well as product template?

Yes! The wizard lets you switch between analyzing individual product variants or rolling them up by product template.

5. What export formats are supported?

You can view results directly inside Odoo using a native pivot table, or click "Export as xlsx" to download a fully styled Excel spreadsheet complete with KPI summary cards.

6. Can I configure default settings so the wizard opens pre-filled?

Yes! Under Settings > Sales > Low Sales Report, you can set the Default Report Type, Default Critical Level, and Default Analyzed Period.

7. Is free support included after purchase?

Yes! Every purchase includes lifetime free support and updates for your Odoo version — no subscriptions, no hidden fees. Just email support@uncannycs.com anytime.
